## Caveats

- **Komunalni prispevek is the least precise number.** The odloki charge on bruto floor area (BTP), not neto; we converted 150 m2 neto to 180 m2 BTP. Odlok prices are mostly 2019–2021 and many municipalities index them at assessment time (vsevem estimates +8 % to +33 %), so actual decisions are likely higher than the `calc_600_180` figures. Zones (Koper), Fn for houses (Ljubljana, Maribor, Celje, Kranj unconfirmed) and local reliefs change the result. A new Uredba (53/26) applies from 28. 3. 2026; its effect on existing odloki was not checked. Uradni list RS returned HTTP 503 during research, so Ljubljana, Maribor, Celje, Kranj and Novo mesto values come from vsevem.si's transcription of the odloki, not the gazette itself (two of its samples were recomputed and matched exactly). Maribor's psz article was deleted in 2024, so its figure is uncertain.
- **Market prices (documentation, connections, slab, earthworks, landscaping)** come from contractor portals (omisli, primerjam, mojmojster, glog) with undisclosed samples; most do not say whether DDV is included. Treat them as ranges, not quotes.
- **Which makers include DGD/PZI or the slab:** no current maker-specific source was found. Only a 2021 forum post (Marles: PID + PZI ~3,500 EUR) and a generic MojMojster statement that prefab prices to the 3rd phase include documentation. The site's own model data ("brez temeljne plošče") is the better signal for the slab.
- **Electricity:** the ELES tariff is official, but it is only the connection-power fee. The physical connection works (cable, meter cabinet) were not found in an official price list.
- **Not found or too rough to show:** fibre and gas connection costs, fence prices (no Slovenian source), MKČN installation cost, and the 9.5 % DDV treatment of a kit delivered without assembly.
- **Eko sklad** requires a passive-house level building (PHPP, n50 ≤ 0.6, rainwater tank, PV); many standard prefab models will not qualify without upgrades. The subsidy cannot exceed 30 % of eligible costs, which in a turnkey contract are at most half the contract value.
