## Caveats

- **Komunalni prispevek is the least precise number.** The odloki charge on bruto floor area (BTP), not neto; we converted 150 m2 neto to 180 m2 BTP. Assessments use the odlok prices: the six odloki read in full (Ljubljana, Koper, Celje, Kranj, Novo mesto, Ptuj) have no indexation clause, and neither ZUreP-3 nor Uredba 53/26 (in force 28. 3. 2026) adds one. Zones (Koper) and local reliefs change the result. Ljubljana, Koper, Celje, Kranj and Novo mesto were confirmed from the odloki via PISRS or Wayback snapshots, because Uradni list RS returned HTTP 503. Maribor is the weakest figure: its 2024 amendment set psz to 100 % per news reports, but the MUV text could not be read, and Fn for houses is unconfirmed.
- **Portal headline prices were too low.** A second check against price lists and real quotes (2026-10-19) found most portal figures low or incomplete; documentation, connections, earthworks and landscaping now use itemised price lists and quotes where available. Telecom connection (300–800) and the electricity connection build (1,500–3,500) are rough.
- **Market prices (documentation, connections, slab, earthworks, landscaping)** come from contractor portals (omisli, primerjam, mojmojster, glog) with undisclosed samples; most do not say whether DDV is included. Treat them as ranges, not quotes.
- **Which makers include DGD/PZI or the slab:** Lumar and Jelovica say DGD+PZI are included (lumar-vkljuceno, jelovica-paket); for the others no current maker-specific source was found. Only a 2021 forum post (Marles: PID + PZI ~3,500 EUR) and a generic MojMojster statement that prefab prices to the 3rd phase include documentation. The site's own model data ("brez temeljne plošče") is the better signal for the slab.
- **Electricity:** the ELES tariff is official, but it is only the connection-power fee. The physical connection works (cable, meter cabinet) are not in an official price list; the 1,500–3,500 EUR range comes from Elektro Gorenjska's rules and a forum example.
- **Not found or too rough to show:** gas connection costs, a priced source for the telecom connection, and the 9.5 % DDV treatment of a kit delivered without assembly.
- **Eko sklad** requires a passive-house level building (PHPP, n50 ≤ 0.6, rainwater tank, PV); many standard prefab models will not qualify without upgrades. The subsidy cannot exceed 30 % of eligible costs, which in a turnkey contract are at most half the contract value.
